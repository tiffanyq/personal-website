I grew up in Vancouver, Canada. I now live in San Francisco, where I'm a product manager on Google Maps.
I work on Street View and Immersive View, using 3D ground and aerial imagery to create first-person experiences that help people understand, explore, and remember the world at scale.
I like thinking about how everything makes me feel, but especially how technology makes me feel. Another Browser Tab is my creative practice where I write essays, draw comics, and build websites and games to capture the tabs open in my mind so I can close them out and make room for new ones.
